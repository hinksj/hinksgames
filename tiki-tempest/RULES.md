# TIKI TEMPEST — Official Rules (v1.0)

*Bartenders race to mix the best tiki cocktails before the weather — or the
bar — closes them down. In the spirit of Sushi Go!, with a storm rolling in.*

**Players:** 2–5 &nbsp;•&nbsp; **Time:** 20–40 min &nbsp;•&nbsp; **Ages:** 8+

---

| Mai Tai | 2 Rum + 1 Lime + 1 Orange Liqueur | 6 | 2 |
| Blue Hawaiian | 1 Rum + 2 Pineapple + 1 Blue Curaçao | 6 | 2 |

| Bahama Mama | 1 Rum + 1 Coconut Cream + 1 Pineapple + 1 Grenadine | 6 | 2 |

**Main Deck (135 cards, tiki-mask back):**

- **91 Ingredients** — common: Rum ×20, Pineapple ×18, Coconut Cream ×16,
  Lime ×16; rare: Grenadine ×6, Orange Liqueur ×5, Blue Curaçao ×5, Nutmeg ×5
- **16 Beers** — worthless all game, decisive at the end
- **28 Specials** — Thieving Seagull ×4, Paper Umbrella ×4, Guest Bartender ×3,
  Tiki Torchlight ×3, Pirate Plunder ×3, Tidal Handover ×3, Island Breeze ×3,
  Make It a Double ×3, Last Call ×1, Storm Surge ×1

## Object

Score the most points across **two rounds** of happy hour by serving cocktails.
(Quick pour: 1 round. Full luau: 3 rounds.)

## Setup

1. Shuffle the Recipe Deck; deal **4 recipes face up** in a row — the
   **Drink Menu**.
2. Shuffle the Main Deck; deal each player **5 cards**.
   - If anyone was dealt **Storm Surge**, shuffle it into the bottom half of
     the deck and deal them a replacement (as printed on the card).
3. Youngest bartender pours first. Play passes left.

## Your Turn

### 1. Draw 2
Draw two cards, one at a time, from the Main Deck.
**If you draw Storm Surge, stop** — it plays itself (see below).

### 2. Play up to 2 cards
Each play is one of:

- **Stock your Bar:** put an Ingredient face up in front of you.
- **Shelve a Beer:** put a Beer face up in your beer row. It stays there for
  the whole game.
- **Play a Special:** resolve its text, then discard it.

**Serving is free** and doesn't count against your 2 plays: at any moment
during your turn, if the ingredients on **your Bar** match a recipe on the
**Drink Menu**, discard those ingredients, take the recipe card, and place the
cocktail face up in your **Served row** — it's now worth its printed points.
Refill the Menu from the Recipe Deck. Serve as many drinks as your Bar can
cover. (**Make It a Double** is played *at the moment you serve* — it also
doesn't count against your 2 plays.)

There is no hand limit and no discard at end of turn.

## The Specials

Text as printed; clarifications govern the edge cases.

| Card | Printed effect | Clarification |
|---|---|---|
| **Thieving Seagull** | Take any Ingredient Card off the table. | Any ingredient on any player's Bar (including yours) goes into **your hand**. Bars under a Paper Umbrella are protected. |
| **Paper Umbrella** | Protects from seagulls. Adds a point to cocktails. Once played, must remain. | Play it onto your Bar. While it's there, Seagulls can't touch **any** of your Bar ingredients. When you next serve a cocktail, the umbrella garnishes it: **+1 point**, and the umbrella stays on that drink (protection ends; a new umbrella can be played). It can never be discarded or moved. |
| **Guest Bartender** | You may demand a specific ingredient from one player. If they have it, that card is now yours. | Name an ingredient and a player; they must hand one over **from their hand** if they hold it. Go fish. |
| **Tiki Torchlight** | Reveal 3 cards from the main deck and add one to your hand | The other two go to the discard pile, face up. If Storm Surge is revealed, it triggers immediately. |
| **Pirate Plunder** | Steal a random card from an opponent's hand. | Then the opponent draws a replacement from the deck (per the designer's card explanations). |
| **Tidal Handover** | Each player passes one card from their hand to the player on their right | Everyone chooses secretly, then all pass at once. Empty hands pass nothing. |
| **Island Breeze** | All players draw 2 cards from the main deck | You included. |
| **Make It a Double** | Play when a cocktail is "served" to double its points. | Doubles the printed value (umbrella point added after doubling). One Double per cocktail. |
| **Last Call** | This is the last round of play — tell the patrons to drink up. | Played from your hand as a normal play. The current round becomes the game's final round, even if more were scheduled. |
| **Storm Surge** | Must be played immediately when drawn and ends the round. | The round ends the instant it's drawn — remaining plays are lost. Once it has struck, it's removed from the game (the next round's sky is clear). |

## Round End

A round ends when **any** of these happens:

- **Storm Surge** is drawn;
- every player has taken **10 turns** (the bar keeps a tab — it's shown online);
- the Main Deck runs out (shuffle the discard pile to refill it once per
  round; the second time it empties, the round ends).

Score served cocktails (with Doubles and Umbrella garnishes) onto the running
total. Then everything resets **except**: your **Beer row** (it persists all
game), the running score, and consumed recipe cards (they're gone — the Menu
runs thinner in later rounds). Unserved Bar ingredients and hands are shuffled
back into the Main Deck along with used specials. Deal new 5-card hands; the
player left of last round's starter pours first.

## Game End & Beer Score

After the final round (round 2 by default, or the Last Call round):

- **Most beers: +10 points.** &nbsp; **Fewest beers (including zero): −5.**
  Ties share the prize/penalty in full. With 2 players, only the +10 applies.
- Highest total is crowned **Master Mixologist of the Tiki Tempest**. Tie:
  most cocktails served; still tied, split the tab and share the title.

## Designer's Notes (what v1.0 fixed)

The deck was designed and printed in 2024; the mechanics were left incomplete.
Decisions made to finish it, and why:

1. **Draw-based turns, not pick-and-pass.** The first sketch was Sushi Go's
   drafting, but the printed cards reference drawing from a deck, stealing
   from hands, and playing cards "off the table" — so v1.0 is draw 2 / play 2
   with free serves, which every printed card supports.
2. **The Drink Menu** (4 face-up recipes, own card back as printed) makes
   cocktails a contested race, replacing the draft's private recipe cards.
3. **Rounds are 10 turns each, 2 rounds standard** — matches the printed
   "round" language on Storm Surge and Last Call and lands the 20–40 minute
   target. Storm Surge cuts a round short; the reshuffle-if-dealt rule is
   printed on the card itself.
4. **Beers score +10/−5** (most/fewest), per the design conversation's
   pudding analogy. They persist across rounds exactly like Sushi Go puddings.
5. **The Seagull/Umbrella duel**: the printed Seagull takes table ingredients,
   so the printed Umbrella's protection covers your Bar while unattached, then
   garnishes (+1) the next drink you serve — honoring all three printed
   clauses including "once played, must remain."
6. **Pirate Plunder's replacement draw** comes from the card-explanations doc
   ("the opponent draws a new card from the deck").
7. **Balance knobs** for playtesting: turns per round (10), rounds (2),
   plays per turn (2), beer bonus (+10/−5).
