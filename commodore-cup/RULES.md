# COMMODORE CUP — Official Rules (v1.0)

*A 1980s yacht-club card game of melds, martinis, and members. First skipper to
50 points hoists the burgee and becomes **Commodore of the Club**.*

**Players:** 2–6 &nbsp;•&nbsp; **Time:** 30–45 min &nbsp;•&nbsp; **Ages:** 12+

---

## Components (84 cards)

**The Club Deck (59 cards, sunset card back)** — the draw pile:

- **48 Suit cards** in four suits, each lettered **A through L**:
  - **Theme Parties** (pink letters): Admiral's Ball, Bollywood Bash, Casino
    Night, Disco Dance-Off, Epicurean Extravaganza, Fiesta Fundraiser, Gatsby
    Gala, Halloween Hoopla, Ice Cream Social, Jazz Party, Karaoke Night, Luau
    Night
  - **Yachts** (white letters — the fleet, each vessel named for a sea
    creature): *Albatross, Barracuda, Clownfish, Dolphin, Egret, Flying Fish,
    Grouper, Hermit Crab, Ibis, Jellyfish, Kraken, Lobster*
  - **Cocktails** (white letters): Aperol Spritz, Bloody Mary, Caipirinha,
    Dark 'n' Stormy, Espresso Martini, French 75, Gin Fizz, Hot Toddy, Irish
    Coffee, Jalapeño Margarita, Kentucky Mule, Lemon Drop
  - **Cruise-Outs** (blue letters): Antigua, BVIs, Catalina Island, The Delta,
    Ensenada, French Polynesia, Grand Cayman, Half Moon Bay, Isle of Wight,
    Juan de Fuca, Key West, La Paz
- **11 Special Action cards** (one of each — see table below)

**The Member Deck (25 cards, club-photo card back)** — the club's colorful
membership, kept as a separate face-down pile. Members carry point bonuses or
penalties plus a one-time effect. Shuffle it **once**: the same pile serves the
entire game and is never reshuffled — when the membership runs out, it runs out.

---

## Object of the Game

Becoming Commodore takes two things: **points** and **the membership behind
you**. Score points by melding suit cards, playing special actions, and
courting members — and win by being the first to hold **50 points AND the
support of at least 5 members** at the end of a round. (Short cruise: play to
25 points. Around-the-world: play to 100.)

## Setup

1. Shuffle the Club Deck. Deal **7 cards** to each player.
2. Place the rest face down as the **draw pile**; flip the top card face up
   beside it to start the **discard pile**. (If it's a Special Action card,
   bury it in the deck and flip another.)
3. Shuffle the Member Deck and set it face down within reach.
4. Whoever most recently stepped off a boat goes first. Play passes to the left.

## Your Turn

Take these phases in order:

### 1. Draw
Take **one card**: the top of the draw pile **or** the top of the discard pile.
(If you take from the discard pile, you may not discard that same card this
turn.)

### 2. Special Action *(optional)*
You may play **one** Special Action card from your hand. Resolve its text, then
place it face up in your **played-actions row** (it is *not* discarded — it
scores for you at round end).

### 3. Meld *(optional, any number)*
Lay down from your hand, face up in front of you:

- **A Set:** 3 or 4 cards of the **same letter** in different suits
  (e.g. G-Gatsby Gala + G-Grouper + G-Gin Fizz).
- **A Run:** 3 or more **consecutive letters** in the **same suit**
  (e.g. C→D→E of Cocktails: Caipirinha, Dark 'n' Stormy, Espresso Martini).
  Runs do not wrap (L→A is not legal).
- **Extensions:** add matching cards to any meld already on the table — yours
  **or another player's**. (A card added to someone else's meld still scores
  for **you**: keep it in your own area, turned sideways, pointing at the meld
  it extends — or online, the game tracks it for you.)

### 4. Membership Drive *(optional, push your luck)*
If you melded anything this turn — a **new set or run**, or a card **added to
any meld on the table** — you **may** draw the top card of the Member Deck:

- Reveal it and place it face up in your **member row**. Its points (from **+8**
  down to **−6**) count toward your score at the end of *this* round — good
  luck at the bar.
- Resolve its effect text **immediately** (draws, discards, peeks, skips,
  swaps). Effects are mandatory.
- **One member draw per new set or run** you lay down — a turn with two fresh
  melds earns two Drives (court after each). A turn with only extensions earns
  one.
- **Members are for keeps.** The card stays in front of you for the rest of the
  game — your **Club Support**. Nothing can take a member from you, and even
  Seasick Susie's vote counts: she costs you 6 points the round you court her,
  but she *will* second your nomination.

Roughly 60% of the membership helps you; the rest are, well, *characters*.
You can't become Commodore without the members' support, and there are only 25
of them for the whole game — brave the deck too late and the votes may be gone.

### 5. Discard
End your turn by placing **one card** from your hand on the discard pile.

**Going out:** if at any point in your turn your hand becomes **empty** — after
melding, or after your discard — the round **ends immediately**. You earn the
**Commodore's Toast** bonus (+5). You may go out without a final discard if
your last cards all meld.

**Empty draw pile:** shuffle the discard pile (except its top card) into a new
face-down draw pile and carry on. The **second** time the draw pile runs dry in
a round, the round ends where it stands — score it, no Toast bonus. If the
Member Deck empties, no more members can be courted this round.

## Special Action Cards

Card text as printed. Clarifications in the right column govern edge cases.

| Card | Printed effect | Clarification |
|---|---|---|
| **Party Foul** | Discard one extra card. | Choose **any player** (yourself included); they immediately discard one card of their choice. |
| **Smooth Talker** | Draw 2 cards. | From the draw pile. |
| **Reciprocity** | Use club reciprocals to steal a card from another player's hand. | Take one card at random (unseen) from an opponent's hand. |
| **Harbor Mixup** | Swap hands with another player. | Entire hands, as they stand. |
| **Fashion Statement** | The next player must skip a turn. | The player to your left misses their next turn. |
| **Member Discount** | Take a card from the discard pile. | Any card in the pile, not just the top. |
| **Dockside Gossip** | Look at the top 3 cards of the draw pile and keep one, discard 2. | If fewer than 3 remain, look at what's there. |
| **Private Party** | All players pass one card to their left. | Everyone (you included) chooses a card simultaneously, then passes. |
| **Sunset Cruise** | All players pass one card to their right. | Same, to the right. |
| **Favorable Winds** | Draw 2 cards from the deck. | From the draw pile. |
| **Against the Current** | Choose a player to draw 2 cards from the deck. | Dead weight in their hand — or a gift; your call. |

*A player with no cards in hand neither passes nor discards from effects.*

## Scoring a Round

When someone goes out, everyone scores:

| Item | Points |
|---|---|
| Each suit card you melded (sets, runs, extensions) | **+1** |
| Completing a set's 4th card | **+2** to whoever added it |
| Each run card beyond the third | **+1** to whoever played it |
| Each Special Action card you played | **+1** |
| Each member courted **this round** | **its printed value** (+8 … −6) |
| Commodore's Toast (you went out) | **+5** |
| Each card left in your hand — suit card | **−1** |
| Each card left in your hand — Special Action card | **−2** |

Members you courted keep their points whether good or bad — the club has a
long memory.

## Winning

Add round scores to a running total (totals can go negative — see: bar tab).
At the end of a round, any player with **50+ points** *and* **5+ members of
Club Support** qualifies for the Commodore's chair; the qualified player with
the most points takes it (ties broken by supporters, then a sail-off round).
At a **six-player** table 25 members can't back everyone, so the bar drops to
**4 supporters** — the membership becomes a race.

Points without support get you nothing but a bar bill — if you cross 50 with
fewer than 5 supporters, the election is postponed and play continues until
someone has both. Court those members.

**Landslide:** at the finish, every supporter **beyond** the required 5 is
worth **+1** — the membership rewards a broad coalition, so the member deck
stays worth fighting over to its last card.

## Designer's Notes (what v1.0 fixed)

The deck was designed and printed in July 2024; the mechanics were left
incomplete. This ruleset finalizes them:

1. **Hand size is 7** (not the drafted 10): the printed Club Deck is 59 cards,
   and 6 players × 7 = 42 leaves a healthy draw pile.
2. **The Member Deck is separate** — the printed cards have two different
   backs, which settles the drafts' ambiguity about whether members shuffle
   into the main deck.
3. **Courting members is optional and once per turn** — the drafts made it
   automatic; making it a choice turns the negative members into a
   push-your-luck decision instead of pure bad luck.
4. **Card text on the printed cards is canon** where drafts and spreadsheet
   disagree (e.g. Podium Pete is +5/peek as printed, not the spreadsheet's
   +10/draw; Starboard Steve is +2/hand-a-card, not −2/skip).
5. **Party Foul** is printed as "Discard one extra card"; the design intent in
   the spreadsheet was "forces a discard." The rule — target any player,
   including yourself — honors both readings: aim it at an opponent to strip a
   future meld card, or at yourself to race toward going out.
6. **Specials score +2 and stay in front of you** — rewarding use over
   hoarding, and Member Discount can't fish spent actions back.
7. **Play to 50** (not 100): with 1-point suit cards, typical round scores run
   10–20, so 50 lands the advertised 30–45 minutes.
8. **The membership elects you** — a 2024 design goal ("to become Commodore
   you should need not only points but the support of members") that the
   drafts never wired up. Club Support (members courted, minimum 5 to win)
   makes the member deck mandatory but *when* to brave it strategic — and
   means the negative members are votes too.
9. **Members are collected, not reshuffled** (playtest, July 2026): courted
   members stay on the table all game and the 25-card deck depletes, so the
   membership is a visible, contested resource. Extending a meld also arms
   the Membership Drive — late-round turns still earn courting rights.
10. **Courting scales with melding** (playtest): originally capped at one
   member per turn; now each new set or run earns its own Drive. Simulation
   shows double-meld turns are rare enough that the aggregate balance doesn't
   move — but the turn where you lay two melds deserves two knocks on the
   member deck.
11. **v1.1 balance** (simulated playtest): specials dropped from +2 to +1 —
   at +2 the winner was farming ~19 points just for firing them, rivaling
   melds; their effects are the real reward. Meld **size bonuses** (+2 for a
   set's 4th card, +1 per run card past the third) reward building the harder
   shapes. **Landslide** keeps courting alive after the fifth vote.
